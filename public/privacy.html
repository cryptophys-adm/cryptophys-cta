Privacy

Privacy policy

What we keep, why, and what we never do.

Last updated 20 November 2026.

Who we are

Cryptophys publishes educational XAUUSD (gold) analysis on this site and on its own social accounts. This policy covers this site (cta.cryptophys.work) and the Cryptophys CTA app, the tool our team uses to prepare, approve and publish those posts.

Visitors to this site

You can read this site without an account. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies (the TradingView widget described below may set its own), and we do not build profiles of visitors. When you follow one of our tracked links, we count the click per link, channel and day; we do not store your IP address, device or any other detail about you.

The live gold price

The home page shows a live gold price (XAUUSD) in a small widget served by TradingView, loaded from TradingView's own site once the page has loaded. TradingView may set its own cookies and receives the usual request details for that widget, under its own privacy policy. We receive nothing from it.

Social accounts we connect

The CTA app connects only to Cryptophys's own accounts on the platforms we publish to (Telegram, Threads, Instagram, X, YouTube and TikTok), through each platform's official login. For each account we keep the access tokens the platform issues, encrypted, and the account's id and display name, so that the app can publish the posts our team approves and read their public metrics (views, likes, comments, shares). We do not read, store or use anyone else's data from these platforms, and we do not access followers' profiles or messages.

What we publish

Every post is prepared from our own analysis, checked automatically, and approved by a person on our team before it is published. We publish only to the accounts we own.

Our team

Team members sign in to the studio through our identity provider. We keep their email address and their actions in the studio (drafts edited, approvals, publications) to run the studio and keep an audit trail.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data. Posts go only to the platform they are published on. Our infrastructure runs on Cloudflare, which processes requests on our behalf.

Retention and removal

Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected in the studio, which deletes them; you can also revoke our app's access in the platform's own settings at any time. Drafts, publication records and metrics are kept for the history of our posts.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact Cryptophys through any of its official channels linked on this site.
