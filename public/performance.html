    R
    What one plan risks. A loss at the stop is −1R.
    Win rate
    The share of plans that ended in profit. "Even" plans closed where they opened, after the stop moved there.
    Expectancy
    The average result of one plan, in R.
    Net R
    All the results added up, in R.
    Profit factor
    Total gains divided by total losses. Above 1, the gains outweighed the losses.
