    From a closed candle to a post

    1. A candle closes

      The 15-minute room meets four times an hour. The hourly, 4-hour and daily rooms meet when their candle closes.

    2. The leader brings it to the table

      Each timeframe has a leader. Sixteen analysts each argue from their own indicator panel, and only from it.

    3. They answer each other

      Analysts who disagree say so. The Moderator weighs the exchange and writes the conclusion you read in the minutes.

    4. Research adds the wider picture

      The dollar, US yields, silver, oil and the economic calendar are read by their own desk.

    5. A post is drafted and checked

      The CTA Editor drafts it, the Content Guardian checks it against the rules, and a person approves it.

    6. It is posted on Threads

      No prices, no levels and no calls to trade go out. What the system did with a meeting is shared only as a recap, a day later.
