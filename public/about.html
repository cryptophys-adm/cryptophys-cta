How it works

From analysis to a post

Every post starts as numbers from the Cryptophys system. Some are worded by a model from those numbers only, then checked by the same rules; a person approves each post or sets the rule that approves it.

  1. 1

    The system reads the market

    The Cryptophys system follows XAUUSD on several timeframes. It maps key levels from established metrics (pivots, Camarilla levels, VWAP and others), recognises chart patterns on H1, H4 and D1, and keeps a record of its own decisions.

  2. 2

    A draft from a fixed template

    A draft is filled from fixed templates with those numbers only: the zones and the metrics behind them, a pattern's lines and its measured projection, or the day's recap. Decisions are shown only as a recap of the last 24 hours.

  3. 3

    Automatic checks

    A guardian checks every draft: no buy or sell call, no entry, stop or target, no promised return, no account data, the disclaimer present, the length right for each channel, and the chart's credit present.

  4. 4

    A person approves, or sets the rule

    A person reads each draft and approves or rejects it, or lets a kind of post be approved automatically once it is ready and checked; replies to comments on our posts go out automatically, checked by the same rules. The checks run again on approval.

  5. 5

    Published on each channel's schedule

    An approved post goes to each channel in its own format, at that channel's posting time, and then appears in the archive.

What each post type means

Key-level map

Zones above and below the price where several metrics agree. A zone is a place to watch, not a place to act.

Chart pattern

A pattern forming or confirmed on H1, H4 or D1, with its lines and the projection measured from its height. Patterns fail often; the projection is a measurement, not a forecast.

Daily recap

What the system decided over the last 24 hours and how it played out, published as a recap, never as a live signal.

What we never post

  • Buy or sell calls, entries, stop losses, take profits or lot sizes
  • Promises of returns or performance claims
  • Account data, positions or anything private
  • News republished from others: only our own short summary, with the source named
  • Price charts drawn from data we have no licence for: our cards show only our own published numbers
