## 2026-11-15
- feat(studio): the performance report and an English studio (owner 2026-11-15: "lanjutkan semua"). Once a day, while publishing is on, the metrics of the last 14 days' posts are read from each platform (`src/insights.mjs`: Threads, Instagram, YouTube; X only when `CONTENT_INSIGHTS` lists it, since X bills reads; Telegram and TikTok have no metrics through the APIs used) and kept in the Desk (`post_metrics`). The studio shows a Performance panel (per channel: posts, views, likes, the best post) and each post's metrics, with a "Read metrics now" button (a person only). Every studio label is now English.
- feat(video): YouTube Shorts and TikTok (owner 2026-11-15: "Youtube & Tiktok"; "lanjutkan semua"). A 9:16 1080 × 1920 video of 14 s made in Browser Run from the draft's card (TradingView's chart with our overlay, the panel): the brand, the card closing in with the draft's own lines one by one, an end card with "Chart by TradingView" and the disclaimer. Recorded by the browser's MediaRecorder (WebM VP9, about 30 fps, about 4 MB); `src/webm.mjs` writes the Duration the recorder leaves out. Kept in the Desk in 1 MB chunks (`media_chunks`), served at `/media/<id>.webm` only once approved, previewed in the studio (CSP `media-src 'self'`). Publishers built: YouTube's resumable upload (refresh-token OAuth; public asked, private until Google verifies the project) and TikTok's Direct Post with FILE_UPLOAD in one chunk (the most public privacy the creator allows; private until TikTok audits the app; the rotated refresh token kept in the Desk, refreshed in the daily run when unused). Video channels get the overlay and TradingView's credit in the caption. Six new secrets (`YOUTUBE_*`, `TIKTOK_*`) in `deploy.secrets_kept`. Verified with a real browser against the live widget: 14.0 s, VP9 1080 × 1920, 393 frames, 4.06 MB, rendered in 22 s.
